LidAndSeek keeps track of what you have stored in boxes, tubs, and bins, so
you can ask "where are my ski goggles?" and get an answer.

## Getting started

1. **Add a location** such as Garage or Basement. Locations can have
   sub-locations (Garage → Shelf B).
2. **Add a container** inside a location and give it a photo.
3. **Add items** to the container. "Add by Camera" lets you photograph
   several items in a row; Smart Naming suggests names from the photos.
4. **Label the container**: create a QR label to print on Avery 22806
   sheets, link a code you already own, or write the label to an NFC tag.
5. **Find things** with Search, by scanning a label, or by asking Siri:
   "Where are my ski goggles in LidAndSeek?"

## Common questions

**Does it sync between my devices?**
Yes, through your private iCloud database when you are signed into iCloud.
Settings shows the sync status. Signing out of iCloud removes the synced
copy from that device; sign back in to restore it.

**Can I get my data out?**
Settings → Export Inventory CSV produces a spreadsheet of every item with
its container, location, and label code.

**Smart Naming suggested the wrong name.**
Suggestions are a starting point. Tap a different chip, or type the name.
On a busy background, the photo's surroundings can influence the guess;
a closer shot helps. Smart Naming can be turned off in Settings.

**Siri doesn't recognize an item.**
Siri learns item names when the app runs. Open LidAndSeek once after adding
items, then try again. Phrases that work: "Where are my … in LidAndSeek",
"Where is the … in LidAndSeek", "Find … in LidAndSeek".

**Scanning doesn't pick up a label.**
Use the flashlight button in low light, move closer, and hold still for a
moment. Damaged or very small codes may need reprinting.

**Which NFC tags work?**
Any writable NDEF tag (NTAG213/215/216 stickers are common and inexpensive).
Write the label from the container's "…" menu.
