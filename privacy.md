Last updated: September 15, 2026

LidAndSeek is an app for keeping track of what you have stored in boxes,
tubs, and bins. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: your inventory belongs to you, it lives on
your devices and in your own iCloud account, and we never see it.

## What the app stores

- The locations, containers, and items you create, including names, notes,
  quantities, and any photos you take or choose.
- Label codes you print or link to containers, and the contents you write
  to NFC tags.
- Your app preferences (for example, whether Smart Naming is on).

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, the
app also keeps a copy in your **private iCloud database** so it syncs to
your other devices and survives a lost or replaced phone. That database is
part of your Apple Account: it is encrypted by Apple, visible only to you,
and not accessible to us. Signing out of iCloud removes the synced copy
from that device.

## What we collect

Nothing. LidAndSeek has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics,
no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. We do not
receive your inventory, your photos, or any identifier for you or your
device.

## Camera, photos, and NFC

- The camera is used to scan container labels and to photograph items.
- Photos you choose from your library are copied into your inventory.
- NFC is used only to read and write the container label tags you choose
  to use.

None of this leaves your device except through your own iCloud sync.

## Smart Naming (Apple Intelligence and Vision)

When Smart Naming is on, the app suggests item names from your photos.
Analysis runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework and,
on supported devices, Apple Intelligence. Photos are never sent to us or to
any naming service. You can turn Smart Naming off in Settings.

## Siri and widgets

The "Find Item" Siri shortcut and the home-screen widget read your
inventory on the device to answer you. Siri processing follows Apple's own
privacy practices; the app does not transmit anything.

## Children

LidAndSeek is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.
